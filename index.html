    Why this ledger exists

    The record is the pressure

    Invisible problems never get fixed. Nashik appears on no “most polluted” list, and still its people breathe air at twice the safe limit — that is how normal bad air has become.

    So a machine reads the government's own sensors every morning at seven, holds them against the standard the WHO says a human body can bear, and writes it down. Publicly. Permanently. Every single day.

    Opinions can be dismissed. A daily record cannot.

    ही नोंद आपल्या पुढच्या पिढ्यांसाठी आहे.

    Method

    Open data · open code

    Source: Central Pollution Control Board real-time monitoring, via the Open Government Data Platform. Readings are pulled daily at 07:00 IST, station values averaged per city, sensor artefacts (≤0 or >1500 µg/m³) discarded, then benchmarked against WHO 2021 24-hour air quality guidelines. Every report is committed to a public repository — the full archive is here, down to the code itself. Nothing to trust; everything to verify.
